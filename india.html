The 2019 assignment

2

The account as pleaded

In 2019 the Petitioner gathered source information as to the availability of evidence against certain fugitive economic offenders who had escaped to Britain — material which, on his case, would unravel a political nexus, shed light on the scam carried out by them while in India, and show how they escaped the process of law here.

Through those sources he was in a position to reach the circle around those persons by presenting himself as a political asylum seeker in Britain. To unravel and expose that nexus and to publish it, he left India for Britain undercover in that guise.

He states that he has since gathered and holds the evidence, and has fulfilled the purpose for which he left. On arrival in India he will place it before the courts and before the prosecuting agencies, including the Central Bureau of Investigation and the Directorate of Enforcement.

His stated intention was never to abscond from the process of law. He approached the High Court for cancellation of the proclaimed-offender status, the non-bailable warrants and the process under Section 82 Cr.P.C., so as to travel to India and join the trial in accordance with law.

Averments. Source: paragraphs 5 to 7 of the petition in CRL.M.C. 5691/2022, on the open record of the High Court; certified copy at e-filing reference 5016978-2025. No court has recorded any finding on these averments.

High Court of Delhi

CRL.M.C. 5691/2022 Disposed of
Cause title
Pushp Sharma v. State of NCT of Delhi
With
CRL.M.A. 22424/2022 and 22426/2022
Arising from
CR Case No. 97507/2016; FIR No. 225/2016, PS Kotla Mubarakpur

Order dated 09.11.2022 (Mendiratta, J.) — Notice issued. The Court recorded observations on the application of Section 82(4) Cr.P.C., which were not in the petitioner’s favour, and left the interim application to be considered after the State’s reply.

Order dated 19.12.2022 (Jasmeet Singh, J.) — The Court held that once cancellation of the Section 82 process is sought, the accused has submitted to the jurisdiction of the Court. The petitioner was directed to appear before the Trial Court and seek cancellation, and it was directed that until that application is decided he shall not be arrested in FIR No. 225/2016 or pursuant to the orders dated 10.10.2019 and 29.10.2022. The petition was disposed of in those terms.

The petition was disposed of, not allowed. No order of any court has quashed the proceedings. Both orders are summarised; the certified copies govern.
