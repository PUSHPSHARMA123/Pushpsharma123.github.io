Investigative work

Star News · India News · Tehelka · Headlines Today · Cobrapost. Open any story for what it found and what followed.

Rent a Riot

IPI Award 2011 2010 · Tehelka

The leadership of Sri Ram Sene, recorded agreeing to organise a riot and to vandalise an art exhibition for money.

The story was awarded the International Press Institute Award, Vienna, for Excellence in Journalism, 2011.

Sick Man Walking

Used as evidence by CBI 2010 · Tehelka

The prime accused in the Satyam matter had remained hospitalised for nine months, evading trial even by video conferencing, while the CBI had filed two chargesheets in a Rs 3,300 crore scam and the case stood committed for trial.

I secured admission to the same hospital and recorded him, on camera, fit to stand trial. CBI Hyderabad used the recordings as evidence in court.

If the Pose Holds

CBI inquiry ordered 2012 · Outlook / IBN 7

Police personnel and others recorded on the conduct of the investigation into the disappearance of Swami Shankardev.

The Government ordered a CBI inquiry, which became CBI Case No. RC 2(S)/2013.

The AYUSH RTI story

Produced FIR 225/2016 11 March 2016 · The Milli Gazette

A story founded on a reply obtained under the Right to Information Act concerning the Ministry of AYUSH.

It produced FIR No. 225/2016 — see Delhi (2016) below.

Operation 136

17 media houses 26 March 2018 · Cobrapost

An undercover investigation into paid news, in which media houses were approached to plant items denigrating the opposition. Seventeen media houses were found to be engaged in the practice.

Publication of part of the material was restrained by a learned Single Judge of the Delhi High Court; the Division Bench set that restraint aside. The case is now a study text in Columbia University's Global Freedom of Expression programme.

PAYTM and demonetisation

2018

An investigation into demonetisation and the role of a pro-government start-up.

¶ 3

Public interest litigation

Filed under Article 32 before the Supreme Court of India. No diary numbers are published here.

The Doctrine of Forensic Equality

Filed Articles 14 · 21 · 39-A

The accused's access to the State's forensic material. In every criminal proceeding the forensic process passes through twelve identifiable stages. At each, the State holds complete institutional control and the accused holds no enforceable counterpart.

Twelve stages. Twelve times the State. Zero times the accused.

State — complete forensic control12 / 12
Accused — independent forensic parity0 / 12

The relief sought is a time-limited Expert Committee, chaired by a former Judge, to frame national minimum standards of forensic parity — not the invalidation of any statute.

National Forensic Elimination Database

Filed Articles 14 · 21

A contamination-control framework for the criminal process: a national elimination database, a human chain of custody, and first-responder protocols, so that material introduced by those who handle a scene can be identified and excluded rather than attributed to an accused.

The relief sought is institutional and prospective — the framing of national minimum standards, not the invalidation of any statute.
